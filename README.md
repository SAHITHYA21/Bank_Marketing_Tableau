# Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes marketing campaign data from a bank to understand customer behavior and optimize future campaigns. The dataset covers customer demographics, campaign engagement, and term deposit purchases from May 2017 to November 2019.

## Features
- **Customer Segmentation**: Insights into different customer groups based on job, education, and marital status.
- **Campaign Effectiveness**: Analysis of call duration, contact type, and response rates.
- **Term Deposit Purchases**: Identifying trends in successful term deposit acquisitions.
- **Visualization**: Tableau dashboard for exploring key patterns.

## Data Used
- Customer job type, education, and marital status.
- Contact method (cellular, telephone, unknown).
- Term deposit purchases by year.
- Campaign duration and engagement statistics.

## Key Insights
- 2018 recorded the highest number of term deposit purchases.
- Management professionals had the highest success rate in purchasing term deposits.
- Cellular contact method was more effective than telephone.
- Longer campaign durations tended to yield higher engagement.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau**: Data visualization and dashboard creation.
- **Dataset**: Bank marketing campaign data from 2017 to 2019.
